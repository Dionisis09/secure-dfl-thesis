ok# Outreach Targets 20

Purpose

This is a first practical target list for a Secure Federated Learning Custom R&D Pilot.

Use this list for manual outreach, not automated spam. The best approach is to contact research leads, innovation teams or lab administrators with a short demo offer.

Important positioning

Do not pitch this as a generic blockchain product.

Pitch it as:

"A custom proof-of-concept for secure federated learning with measurable privacy/security overhead, adaptive protection and auditability."

## Outreach note

For universities and labs, lead with collaboration and demo.

For companies and platforms, lead with a paid pilot only after they show interest.

Do not ask for 10,000 EUR in the first message. First goal is a 20-minute demo call.
