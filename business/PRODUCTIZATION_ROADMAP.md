# Productization Roadmap

Goal

Move from thesis prototype to sellable Custom R&D service.

Important positioning

Do not sell the repository as a standalone product.

Sell a custom proof-of-concept service:

"We adapt a secure federated learning prototype to your data and provide measurable evidence about accuracy, privacy/security overhead, communication cost and auditability."

Phase 1 - Demo readiness

Priority:
Very high

Tasks:

- Keep one fast demo command that runs in a few minutes.
- Keep one clean final report.
- Keep generated plots and CSV outputs.
- Prepare a short 5 to 10 minute demo script.
- Prepare a short business pitch.

Success criterion:

The project can be shown to a client without explaining messy internal details.

Phase 2 - Client discovery

Priority:
Very high

Tasks:

- Identify 20 possible contacts.
- Focus on labs, AI startups, healthcare data teams, finance R&D and university groups.
- Send short outreach messages.
- Offer a free 20-minute demo call.

Success criterion:

At least 3 calls booked.

Phase 3 - Custom PoC template

Priority:
High

Tasks:

- Define input data format.
- Define expected deliverables.
- Define timeline.
- Define pricing tiers.
- Define what is excluded.
- Prepare a reusable proposal template.

Success criterion:

A serious client can receive a proposal within 24 hours after the discovery call.

Phase 4 - Technical upgrade if needed

Priority:
Medium

Only implement this after there is real interest.

Possible upgrades:

- Real Ed25519 signatures instead of simulated signatures.
- Better audit CLI.
- Docker setup.
- Cleaner experiment config files.
- Dataset adapter interface.
- One-click report generation.

Success criterion:

Technical work is driven by client demand, not by guessing.

Phase 5 - Paid pilot

Priority:
Very high

Target offer:

10,000 EUR+ Custom R&D package.

Pilot structure:

- 25 percent upfront
- 50 percent after prototype adaptation
- 25 percent after final report and handover

Deliverables:

- Working prototype
- Experiment outputs
- Plots
- Technical report
- Handover meeting
- Recommendations for next phase

Success criterion:

The client pays for a specific use case, not for generic software.

What to avoid

- Do not overbuild a SaaS before demand exists.
- Do not build a production blockchain now.
- Do not claim legal compliance.
- Do not claim medical certification.
- Do not sell it as enterprise-ready.
- Do not hide limitations.

Best next action

Prepare a one-page proposal template and a short demo video or live demo flow.
