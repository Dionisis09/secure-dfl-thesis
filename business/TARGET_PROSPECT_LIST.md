# Target Prospect List

Purpose

This file is a practical outreach map for selling a Custom R&D pilot around secure federated learning.

Best first targets

1. University AI and cybersecurity labs

Why they fit:
They understand research prototypes, privacy-preserving ML and academic reports. They may need prototype support for papers, grants or student projects.

Offer angle:
Secure federated learning proof-of-concept with masking, HE, adaptive security and reproducible report.

2. Healthcare AI research groups

Why they fit:
Healthcare data is sensitive and often cannot be centralized. Hospitals, clinics and research groups may want collaborative ML without sharing raw patient data.

Offer angle:
Evaluate whether federated learning is realistic for multi-site medical data before investing in production.

3. Financial technology and fraud detection teams

Why they fit:
Banks and fintech teams often have distributed sensitive data and strong audit requirements.

Offer angle:
Privacy-preserving collaborative model training with measurable communication, accuracy and security overhead.

4. Industrial IoT and edge AI teams

Why they fit:
Data often lives across devices, factories or sites. Centralization may be expensive or impractical.

Offer angle:
Test decentralized learning across edge clients and measure communication/security trade-offs.

5. AI startups preparing enterprise pilots

Why they fit:
They may need a fast R&D prototype to prove a privacy-preserving AI idea to enterprise clients.

Offer angle:
Four-week custom PoC with technical report and demo outputs.

Search keywords

Use these phrases on LinkedIn, Google and university websites:

- privacy-preserving machine learning lab
- federated learning research group
- healthcare AI research center
- medical AI lab federated learning
- fintech AI fraud detection research
- edge AI industrial IoT research
- applied machine learning privacy
- trustworthy AI lab
- secure machine learning group
- AI cybersecurity lab

People to contact

Ideal roles:

- Professor
- Research Director
- Principal Investigator
- Head of AI
- ML Engineering Lead
- Data Science Lead
- Innovation Manager
- R&D Manager
- CTO in AI startup

Minimum outreach batch

Start with 20 contacts:

- 8 university or research labs
- 4 healthcare AI groups
- 3 fintech or fraud analytics teams
- 3 industrial IoT or edge AI teams
- 2 AI startups

Qualification questions

Ask these during the first call:

- Where is the data currently stored?
- Why can it not be centralized?
- What prediction task matters?
- How many data owners or sites are involved?
- Is the priority privacy, auditability, accuracy or communication cost?
- Do they need a research prototype or production roadmap?
- What would make a four-week PoC successful?

Red flags

Avoid prospects who:

- expect production certification from a small pilot
- want legal compliance guarantees
- cannot define a dataset or use case
- only want free consulting
- want a generic blockchain product
- do not understand why federated learning is relevant

The prototype compares baseline federated learning, masking, selective homomorphic encryption, adaptive security and audit verification, with reproducible metrics and a technical report.

Would it be useful to show you a short demo and see if this fits any of your research or pilot needs?

Best,
[Your Name]
